# Media direction

## Assign a job

Business context communicates who is served and what work feels like. Product evidence demonstrates an actual operation. A connected-mechanism scene explains cause and effect. A breadth gallery demonstrates varied uses. Trust media supports a real claim. Atmosphere adds character without a new factual claim.

“Premium business photo” is not a brief. Specify action, light direction, environment, focal object, colour relationship, negative space and safe overlay zones. An isolated merchandise still can wrongly position a suite as an ecommerce-theme company. Counter with an actual operational relationship or representative second use case—not unrelated stock photos.

## Recompose truthfully

Choose the decisive region: payment state, order relationship, customer action, reconciliation result. Preserve enough surrounding UI to identify the product. Don't ask visitors to read an entire desktop at390px. Match authorized spacing, iconography, typography, terms and behaviour.

Map every visible capability to evidence. If future-facing, label concept. Use synthetic/sanitized data. Primary outcome clear; secondary context legible; peripheral chrome subdued. Masks are editorial crops, not concealment for broken layout. Keep amounts and states intact.

## Asset specification

Record purpose, claim, owner/source, rights, factual/illustrative status, rendered/natural dimensions, desktop/mobile crop, focal point, palette/light, safe zones, motion length, poster, alt/caption/transcript, reduced treatment, loading priority and acceptance captures.

Choose export dimensions from rendered dimensions and expected DPR. Compare actual encoded size/fidelity rather than naming a universal format winner. Use responsive sources where useful and reserve intrinsic space. Prioritize genuinely critical assets; lazy-load below-fold media.

A poster must tell a meaningful story alone. Essential copy/CTAs cannot exist only in video. Generated photography isn't customer proof. Inspect hands, geometry, reflections, edges, shadows and text artifacts where present. Never generate authoritative UI text as bitmap art.

## Reject

Reject media if generic, category-narrowing, implausible, unreadable, inconsistent across scenes, badly cropped, rights-unclear or implying an unverified result.

Linear demonstrates selective emphasis with DOM-heavy product scenes. Figma uses finished work and a controllable custom video hero. Mollie joins finance to recognizable business actions. Retool joins building with data/governance. Transfer the mechanisms, not branded assets or customer claims. Competitor screenshots remain research-only.
