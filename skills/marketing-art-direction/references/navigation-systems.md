# Suite navigation is part of the product argument

Read when specifying/reviewing suite-homepage navigation. A section table of contents is not a substitute for product discovery, integration evaluation and adoption. It can remain separate local navigation.

## Information and destinations

Specify families, task-based groups, concise labels, useful descriptions and actual routes. Separate existing marketing pages, documentation fallbacks, account entry and pages still required. Never invent pricing, availability or placeholder links to match a reference's breadth.

For audience modes maintain stable capability IDs and one destination record. Choose audience-specific language, ordering, proof and emphasis. Switching must not strand focus in replaced DOM. AI/integrations may serve both operators and builders: business jobs versus contracts/tooling, not automatic developer-only placement.

## Expanded material contract

Choose shell-aligned floating surface, full-width continuation or compact disclosure based on content. Specify header attachment, maximum width/height, column measures/gaps, main/secondary fields, type roles, edge hierarchy, radius and shadow. Different families can have different densities in one shell; do not force all menus into an empty grid.

Group ordinary links with spacing/type. A field change can identify the secondary rail; a useful rule can divide a footer. Do not frame each link. Reserve supporting media for a meaningful route, with provenance, crop, failure state and phone decision. Keep text routes when approved media is absent. A menu is not a decorative carousel.

## Behaviour with engineering consequences

Define closed, pending-open, open(category), switching, pending-close, closing and phone root/category states. One logical category is active. Click/keyboard bypass pointer dwell. Cancel stale timers and exit callbacks. Maintain pointer continuity without covering neighbouring controls.

Prefer disclosure buttons and destination anchors. Define focus entry, Escape/outside dismissal, return focus, non-modal desktop versus modal phone, inert background and scroll restoration. Do not add application-menu ARIA to ordinary site links without its keyboard model.

Specify opening, switching and closing independently. A measured-height shared shell can resize without stretching text; animate wrappers separately from contents. Remove exiting links from interaction immediately. Reduced motion preserves instant discovery, not merely a still hero.

Define the content-fit breakpoint, short-screen scrolling, Back/Close and account actions, and resize cleanup. Phone recomposes rather than scales the desktop grid; retain essential destinations.

## Traceability gate

Record evidence → product decision → component/state → acceptance capture/test for defining lessons. Research can mark the final steps pending; implementation review cannot replace them with prose. Require viewed proof of the richest panel, a differently shaped panel, keyboard focus and phone drill-down. Preserve the selected brand, not reference colours/fonts/artwork.
