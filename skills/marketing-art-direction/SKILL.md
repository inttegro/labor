# Marketing Art Direction

For suite navigation, read [navigation systems](references/navigation-systems.md) and specify expanded states, destinations and phone behaviour—not only header labels.

Create a product-specific visual language with engineering consequences. Preserve the selected direction, brand and approved claims. Review or research does not authorize implementation.

Read [composition and type](references/composition-type.md), [material language](references/material-language.md) and [narrative decisions](references/narrative-decisions.md). Use actual rendered reference evidence, not brand stereotypes. The material contract is mandatory, not a final decorative layer.

For a specification request, deliver a direction contract: intended perception, central visual mechanism, hierarchy, density, type roles, media jobs, surfaces, motion purpose, responsive recomposition and observable acceptance conditions. For a critique, evaluate the existing contract and propose only the refinements needed; do not require a new direction document automatically.

A palette, font pair and rounded card system are insufficient. Explain why each focal object exists, how it connects to the next chapter and what can be understood without reading every sentence. Compare equal viewport sizes and equivalent section jobs.

Make the design decisions: recommend a specific surface system and section presentation formats with reasons and rejected alternatives. Do not ask the user to choose a gallery, loop or scroll effect merely because several are possible. Ask only for missing authority, facts or genuinely consequential preference.

Do not convert generic anti-AI advice into bans on the user's preferred colours or serif type. Test whether each choice is doing meaningful work. Proposition, composition, material execution and media truth are separate acceptance gates; strength in one cannot compensate for failure in another. Refine those before micro-spacing.
