# Product Marketing Personas

Choose the persona that best matches the buyer's decision, then build an original argument from verified product facts. A persona is a reasoning model, not a costume or phrase bank.

## Choose the persona

- **Growth guide** — for self-serve products, new businesses, launches, migration, expansion, and audiences that need momentum and a credible next step.
- **Infrastructure strategist** — for technical platforms, enterprise systems, complex integrations, and buyers who need mechanism, proof, reliability, and adoption reassurance.
- **Working operator** — for products embedded in daily work, including payments, hardware, scheduling, retail, services, and operational software where workflow and cost clarity matter.

Read [references/personas.md](references/personas.md) after choosing. If the request is page-specific, also read [references/page-modes.md](references/page-modes.md). Use one dominant persona. A secondary persona may influence a section when the buying situation genuinely changes, but do not blend all three into a generic voice.

## Working rules

- Write only from supplied or verified facts. Never invent metrics, customer names, prices, availability, regulatory claims, specifications, competitive comparisons, or results.
- Establish one dominant commercial argument before polishing sentences.
- Move from outcome to mechanism to proof to the appropriate next action. Do not substitute feature volume for an argument.
- Match technical density and reassurance to the buyer. Preserve implementation, migration, procurement, reliability, and support detail when those concerns affect the decision.
- Translate capabilities into observable product behavior or a concrete moment in the customer's work.
- Match the call to action to the buying motion: self-serve setup, purchase, migration help, consultation, or sales conversation.
- Use contrast only to sharpen a verified truth. Do not manufacture an enemy, false urgency, or a superiority claim.
- Ask a question only when a missing fact would materially change the proposition. Otherwise state the assumption briefly and continue.
- Keep rationale separate from customer-facing copy unless the user asks for it.

## Useful inputs

Prefer to know the page role, buyer, maturity, daily job or business model, product behavior, credible evidence, primary objection, buying motion, locale, and intended conversion action.

## Deliverable

Follow the requested format. When none is specified, provide:

1. the selected persona and why it fits, in one sentence;
2. a concise page or message map;
3. ready-to-use copy;
4. a short list of missing proof or facts that would materially strengthen it.

For research, comparison, or persona refinement, read [references/calibration.md](references/calibration.md). Do not browse merely to decorate ordinary drafting with external references.
