# Research and calibration

## Build a diverse evidence corpus

When refining a persona, study several current public examples that solve the same communication problem. Include different companies, buyer sizes, regions, and product categories so that one brand's temporary campaign does not become a universal rule.

Record separately:

- directly observed structure, claims, language, and interaction;
- interpretation of the decision behind each observation;
- the transferable principle proposed from repeated evidence;
- time-sensitive facts that must not become permanent guidance.

Do not treat public wording as a phrase bank. Preserve the decision logic and produce original language.

## Blind calibration

Give the skill a brief containing the product, page role, audience, facts, proof, conversion goal, and constraints without revealing the reference page's headline, section order, screenshots, URL, or distinctive phrases.

Evaluate:

1. proposition and information hierarchy;
2. persona selection;
3. outcome-to-mechanism reasoning;
4. proof and objection handling;
5. feature selection and page pacing;
6. call-to-action fit;
7. factual restraint.

After revealing the reference, compare decisions rather than phrase overlap. A strong result should reproduce useful reasoning, not recognizable expression.

## Refresh boundary

Revisit the corpus when current prices, offers, specifications, product facts, comparative claims, or buyer expectations matter. Do not silently promote temporary campaigns, experiments, or local rates into stable persona guidance.
