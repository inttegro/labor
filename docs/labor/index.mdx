## What is available today

- **22 public skills** with independently installable `SKILL.md` entrypoints.
- **78 routing cases** covering positive, negative, ambiguous, and multi-skill requests.
- **Deterministic validation** that runs without provider credentials or a model call.
- **Explicit provenance** for Inttegro-authored and third-party-derived material.
