## What you get

- **22 installable skills** with focused `SKILL.md` entrypoints.
- **78 routing cases** across direct, irrelevant, ambiguous, and multi-skill requests.
- **Local validation** for the catalog and test corpus, with no model call required.
- **Recorded provenance** for original and adapted material.
